How it works

From a phone call to a book on the shelf

Built for sons and daughters abroad who want their parents' and grandparents' stories before they are lost.

  1. Record or upload

    Record in the app, or upload a voice note or call recording you already have. You can also type or paste a story.

  2. Get the transcript

    The story is written out in the language it was told, with tone marks. You can correct any word.

  3. Read it in English

    An English translation sits beside the original, so cousins who don't speak the language can follow along.

  4. Make the storybook

    Collect the stories into a PDF family storybook to share, print and pass down.
