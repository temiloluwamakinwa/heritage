# Translation comparison samples (Claude vs Gemini)

These are SYNTHETIC test stories I wrote, not real recordings, and my Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa is imperfect. Use them only to try the workflow. Replace them with 2-3 real transcripts from your elders for the real decision.

## How to compare
1. Paste each story into the Gemini app with the prompt below.
2. Paste the same story to me; I translate with Claude using the same prompt.
3. Check both against a fluent family member. Score: meaning kept, names kept, proverbs glossed, unclear parts marked [?], nothing invented.

Prompt: "Translate this family story into English. Keep personal names and place names as written. Gloss proverbs briefly in brackets. Mark anything unclear with [?]. Do not add details that are not in the text."

## 1. Yoruba
Ní ìgbà tí mo wà ní ọmọdé, baba ńlá mi máa ń sọ ìtàn fún wa lábẹ́ igi ọ̀pẹ ní Ìbàdàn. Ó máa ń sọ pé, "Ọmọ tí a kò kọ́ ni yóò gbé ilé tà."

## 2. Pidgin
Wen I be small pikin for Warri, my grandmama go dey cook for fire every evening. She go tell us say, "Wetin dey for yam no dey for water yam," and everybody go laugh.

## 3. Hausa
Lokacin da nake yaro a Kano, kakata ta kan ba mu labari da dare. Ta ce, "Hakuri maganin duniya."
